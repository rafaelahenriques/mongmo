Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

This is what the Mongmo app collects about you, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It describes what the app actually does, not what it might do.

Who we are

Mongmo, Jan Palfijnstraat 26, 8500 Kortrijk, Belgium, is responsible for your data (the controller). Questions or requests: hello@mongmo-store.com.

What we collect, and why

Your account: your email address and password (stored hashed, never readable, by our sign-in provider), the name you choose, and, if you add them, a profile photo and your home city. Guests have no email or password. Why: to run your account (our contract with you).

Your orders: what you ordered, where and when you pick it up, the price and any reward you used. Why: to make and hand over your order (contract).

Paying: at the counter when you collect your order, or in the app. When you pay in the app, Stripe's payment screen takes your card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Bancontact details and sends them straight to Stripe: we never see or store them. We keep whether and when an order was paid, Stripe's reference for the payment, and any refund. Why: to take payment for your order and refund it if it's cancelled (contract), and to keep the accounts the law requires.

Points, levels, badges and rewards: what you earned, from what, and what you redeemed. Why: the loyalty programme you join by signing up (contract).

Check-ins and challenges: which pop-up you checked in at and when, and the result of each challenge (a score, a rating, a vote, how many photos). Your location is used once to check you're at the pop-up and is not stored. Why: the programme (contract).

Photos you take for a challenge: stored privately; only you and Mongmo staff can see them. Right after you take one, we ask whether to share it in Photos of the week. If you say yes, other members can see it on Home for seven days, without your name, and you can stop sharing at any time (Account → Your photos). Anyone can report a shared photo: it's taken down at once and staff look at the report. Why: the photo challenge you chose to do; sharing only with your consent.

Your profile photo, if you add one (Account → Edit profile): stored privately; only you can see it, and other members while you're on the leaderboard. Change or remove it any time: a removed or replaced photo is deleted. Any member can report a profile photo: it's hidden from the leaderboard at once (you see a note about it) and staff decide whether it comes back or is removed; who reported it isn't shown and the report is deleted once decided. Why: your choice (consent); reports, to keep the leaderboard safe (legitimate interest).

The leaderboard: only if you join it (Achievements → Leaderboard), your name, profile photo (if you added one) and points are shown to other members. You can leave it any time in Settings. Why: your consent.

Crash reports, only if you said yes: when the app crashes, a report of what went wrong in the code, your phone model, system and app version. No name, email, location, or anything you typed. Why: your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings.

What stays on your phone

Nearby pop-up alerts use your phone's location in the background to notice when you're close to a pop-up; that happens on your phone and isn't sent to us. Notifications are created on your phone; we don't collect a push token. Your cart, settings and a copy of recent data (so the app opens offline) are kept on your phone. Your sign-in is kept in the phone's secure storage (Keychain or Keystore).

Who else handles it

Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage, as our processor.

Sentry receives crash reports, only if you said yes, as our processor.

Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ireland) handles payments in the app, as our payment provider. Stripe also uses payment data under its own privacy policy, for example to prevent fraud and to meet its legal duties (stripe.com/privacy).

Expo (650 Industries, United States) delivers small fixes to the app between store updates, as our processor. When the app opens, it asks Expo's server whether there is a newer version. The request carries your phone's system (iOS or Android), the app's version and a random token for this installation: not your account, and no device identifier. Expo is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Apple and Google run the app stores, your phone's location services, and the maps app that opens when you ask for directions.

Staff tools only: when Mongmo staff search for an address for a new pop-up, the search text goes to Photon (komoot) to find it.

We don't sell your data, use advertising networks, or track you across other apps and websites.

How long we keep it

Unpaid draft orders: 1 day. Guest accounts not used: 30 days. Challenge photos: 12 months. Your profile photo: until you change or remove it. Check-ins, challenge results and orders: 24 months. Rewards you've used: 24 months after you used them. Payment records with each order: as long as the order (24 months), or longer where accounting law requires. Points history: as long as your account. Accounts not used for 24 months are deleted entirely. Crash reports: 30 days. Deletion happens automatically, every night.

Your rights

See and download your data: Settings → Your data → Download a copy.

Correct it: Account → Edit profile, and Settings for your email and password.

Delete it: Settings → Delete account. Your account, orders, points, badges, rewards, check-ins, challenge results and photos are deleted from our systems, not hidden.

Withdraw consent: crash reports and the leaderboard in Settings, a shared photo in Account → Your photos, your profile photo in Account → Edit profile, any time.

You can also object to or ask us to limit how we use your data, by email. If you're unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (www.dataprotectionauthority.be).

Security

Everything is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules mean each person can only reach their own data. Passwords are hashed. Your sign-in on the phone is kept in its secure storage. To stop misuse, we count how often an account starts a payment or a refund in a minute; the counts are deleted after a day.

Children

Mongmo isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this page and its date, and tell you in the app before it applies.
