Who we are

Your account

You need to be 13 or older to use the app. Keep your password to yourself: what happens with your account is your responsibility.

You can also use the app as a guest, without an email. A guest account that isn't used for 30 days is deleted, and any account that isn't used for 24 months is deleted with its points and rewards.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. Your orders, points, rewards and photos are deleted with it.

Ordering and prices

Prices are in euros and include VAT. Each dish's page lists its allergens; if you have an allergy, ask us before you order.

The total you see when you place an order is what you pay, including any reward you use. Your order is placed when you tap Place order; we prepare it for the pickup time you chose.

If we can't make your order (for example because something sold out), we cancel it and you don't pay. A reward you used comes back.

Paying

You pay at the counter when you collect your order.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order in the app until we start preparing it: My orders → the order → Cancel order. A reward you used comes back. Once we've started, it can't be cancelled.

Because food is prepared for a set pickup time, the 14-day right of withdrawal for online purchases doesn't apply to orders (Belgian Code of Economic Law, article VI.53).

Points, levels and rewards

You earn points for orders you collect, for challenges and for badges, as the app shows. Your level follows the points you've earned over time.

Points and rewards have no cash value: they can't be exchanged for money or given to someone else. A reward may have an end date, which it shows.

We may change how many points things earn or cost, and which rewards there are. Points you've already earned stay yours. Points or rewards gained by mistake or by misusing the app may be taken back.

Pop-ups and check-ins

Pop-up times and places can change, so check the app before you go. To check in, you need to be at the pop-up and scan its code; the app checks your location once.

Your photos stay yours. When you share a challenge photo in Photos of the week, or add a profile photo, you allow Mongmo to show it in the app as described: in Photos of the week for seven days, and your profile photo on the leaderboard while you're on it. You can stop either at any time.

Only share photos you took or may share, and don't show other people without their permission. Nothing offensive, hateful, sexual, violent or illegal, nothing that breaks someone else's rights, and no advertising.

Anyone can report a photo. We may hide or remove photos that break these rules, and close accounts that keep doing so.
