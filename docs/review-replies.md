# Replying to reviews

How to answer reviews on the App Store and Google Play, with templates.
Replies are public, so write for everyone who reads them, not only the
person who wrote the review.

## Rules

- **Reply within two working days** to every review of 3 stars or less,
  and to any review that reports a bug. A quick thank-you for good ones is
  enough when there's time.
- **Sign with your first name and "from Mongmo"**: a person, not a
  department.
- **Never** argue, blame the customer, promise a date you can't keep, or
  offer money or free food in a public reply (offer it privately, by
  email).
- **Never** ask for or repeat personal details in a reply: no order
  numbers with names, emails or card details. Ask them to write to
  hello@mongmo-store.com.
- **Never** ask for a better rating in exchange for anything: both stores
  forbid it.
- When a bug is fixed, **reply again** saying which version fixes it; on
  Google Play the reviewer gets a notification and often updates the
  rating.
- Log each bug report in GitHub Issues (`bug` label) with the app version
  and phone from the review, and link the issue in your notes.

## Templates

Fill in the parts in [brackets]. Keep them short: two to four sentences.

**A bug or crash**

> Thanks for telling us, [name], and sorry about that. We're looking into
> [what happened, in their words] now. If you can, write to
> hello@mongmo-store.com with your phone model, so we can make sure the fix
> works for you. — [Your name] from Mongmo

**A bug that's fixed**

> Good news, [name]: version [x.y.z], out now, fixes [the problem]. Thanks
> for helping us find it. — [Your name] from Mongmo

**A problem with an order (late, wrong, cold)**

> We're really sorry your [bánh mì] wasn't right, [name]. Please write to
> hello@mongmo-store.com with the day and the pickup place, and we'll make
> it up to you. — [Your name] from Mongmo

**A payment or refund question**

> Sorry for the worry, [name]. Refunds for cancelled orders go back to how
> you paid and can take a few days to show. If it's been longer than a
> week, write to hello@mongmo-store.com and we'll check it with you
> straight away. — [Your name] from Mongmo

**A missing feature or idea**

> Thanks for the idea, [name]. We've added [the idea] to our list for the
> next updates. — [Your name] from Mongmo

**Privacy or data**

> Thanks for asking, [name]. You can see, download or delete everything
> the app keeps about you in Settings → Your data, and our privacy policy
> is in Settings too. There are no ads and no tracking. — [Your name] from
> Mongmo

**A rude or unfair review**

> Sorry to hear that, [name]. We'd like to understand what went wrong:
> please write to hello@mongmo-store.com. — [Your name] from Mongmo

(Report a review only when it breaks the store's rules: hate speech,
personal details, spam or a review of the wrong app. A bad rating alone
isn't a reason.)

**A good review**

> Thank you, [name]! See you at the next pop-up. — [Your name] from Mongmo
